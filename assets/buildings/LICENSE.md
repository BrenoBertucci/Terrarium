# Building sprites

`mart_facade.png` (and its x6 preview) is drawn by `tools/mart_facade.py`
for this mod. No third-party pixels.

The Pokemon Center used to be voxelized from a fan drawing of an X/Y Center
(`ulithium_poke_center_mart.png`). A fan drawing of a Nintendo design is
still a derivative of that design, and there was no licence from its artist
either, so it was removed from the repository on 2026-09-15. The Center is
built by the classic voxel kit again.

`lavender_materials.png`, `lavender_home.png`, `lavender_civic.png` and
`lavender_ground.png` are swatch sheets and a painted plan written by this
mod's own scripts (`tools/check_lavender_houses.py`, `tools/lavender_home.py`,
`tools/lavender_civic.py`, `tools/lavender_ground.py`). No third-party pixels.
Lavender's Centre is signed with a heart, not with a ball: a Nintendo design
redrawn in voxels would still be that design.

`vermilion_houses.png`, `vermilion_home.png` (`tools/vermilion_home.py`), `fence_kit.png`, `bridge_kit.png`, `reef_kit.png` and the
`vermground_*.png` plans are swatch sheets and painted plans written by this mod's
own scripts (`tools/vermilion_houses.py`, `tools/fence_sheet.py`,
`tools/bridge_sheet.py`, `tools/reef_sheet.py`, `tools/vermilion_ground.py`). No
third-party pixels. Vermilion's houses are signed with a fish, an anchor, a rosette
and two arrows -- no Nintendo mark. Inside the Fan Club a loving cup stands
where the tileset draws a statue of a Pokemon.

`celadon_towers.png` (facade strips, `tools/celadon_towers.py`) and `celground_*.png`
(`tools/celadon_ground.py`) are painted by this mod's own scripts. No third-party
pixels; Celadon's Centre is signed with a heart, its store with the word MART.

`cerground_*.png`, `westground_*.png`, `saffground_*.png`, `fuchground_*.png` and
`cinnground_*.png` are painted plans written by this mod's own scripts
(`tools/cerulean_ground.py`, `west_ground.py`, `saffron_ground.py`,
`fuchsia_ground.py`, `cinnabar_ground.py`), one texel a voxel. No third-party
pixels; the tiles of each map only decided where paving, lawn and flowers lie.
The mosaics are this mod's: a river, ripples, a compass rose, a leaf, an
ammonite, Deco diamonds, a flame.

`town_houses.png` (material strips for the houses of Cerulean and Celadon,
`tools/town_houses.py`) is painted by this mod's own script, texel by texel. No
third-party pixels. The houses are signed with words (BIKE SHOP, PRIZE, CINEMA,
DINER, ON AIR) and with things drawn for this mod: eight plain round medals, a
leaf, a loaf, a green cross, an open book, a record, a coin with a lit diamond,
a donut, a rainbow. No Nintendo mark -- no badge, no ball, no Pokemon.

Do not add fan art or ripped sprites to this folder. `.gitignore` blocks the
old file by name.
